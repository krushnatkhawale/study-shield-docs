As a Parent, I want one tap to play again for the same child, so I do not rebuild the session from scratch.

Persona: Parent.

## Technical context

## Acceptance
1. Success: Already shipped. Play again for the same child is on Home and on results. Do not pick this up.
2. Failure: Auto-start without parent tap. TV-initiated replay.

## Testing requirements
